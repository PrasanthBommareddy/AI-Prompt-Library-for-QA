# Responsible AI for QA Engineers

## What is Responsible AI?

Responsible AI is the practice of designing, developing, deploying, and using AI systems in a way that is fair, safe, transparent, secure, and accountable.

---

# Why It Matters for QA

QA Engineers are responsible for validating AI-generated outputs and ensuring AI behaves reliably.

Responsibilities include:

- Validate AI responses
- Detect hallucinations
- Verify business rules
- Test edge cases
- Check bias
- Protect user data

---

# Google's Responsible AI Principles

- Fairness
- Privacy & Security
- Safety
- Accountability
- Transparency

---

# QA Validation Checklist

- Verify AI output accuracy
- Validate business requirements
- Test negative scenarios
- Check prompt quality
- Detect hallucinations
- Review bias
- Protect sensitive information
