# Responsible AI for QA Engineers

## What is Responsible AI?

Responsible AI is the practice of designing, developing, deploying, and using AI systems in a way that is fair, safe, transparent, secure, and accountable.

---

## Why QA Engineers Should Care

- Validate AI-generated outputs
- Detect hallucinations
- Identify bias
- Protect sensitive data
- Verify AI reliability
- Ensure compliance

---

## Google's Responsible AI Principles

- Fairness
- Privacy & Security
- Safety
- Accountability
- Transparency

---

## QA Checklist

✅ Verify AI responses

✅ Check hallucinations

✅ Test with edge cases

✅ Validate business rules

✅ Protect sensitive data

✅ Review prompt quality
