# AI Glossary for QA Engineers

## Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Simulation of human intelligence by machines.

---

## Machine Learning (ML)

A subset of AI where systems learn from data without explicit programming.

---

## Generative AI

AI capable of creating text, images, code, audio, or videos.

---

## LLM (Large Language Model)

A deep learning model trained on massive text datasets to understand and generate human language.

Examples:

- GPT
- Gemini
- Claude
- Llama

---

## Prompt

The instruction given to an AI model.

---

## Prompt Engineering

The practice of designing prompts to obtain accurate and useful AI responses.

---

## Hallucination

When AI generates incorrect or fabricated information confidently.

---

## Token

The smallest unit processed by an LLM.

---

## Context Window

The maximum amount of information an AI model can process in a single conversation.

---

## Few-shot Prompting

Providing examples before asking AI to perform a task.

---

## Zero-shot Prompting

Giving AI a task without any examples.

---

## Chain of Thought

Encouraging AI to reason step by step.

---

## Embedding

A numerical representation of text used for similarity search.

---

## Vector Database

A database optimized for storing and searching embeddings.

Examples:

- Pinecone
- ChromaDB
- Weaviate
- FAISS

---

## RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Combines external knowledge with LLMs to generate more accurate responses.

---

## Fine-tuning

Training an existing LLM on additional domain-specific data.

---

## AI Agent

An AI system capable of planning, reasoning, and taking actions.

---

## Agentic AI

Multiple AI agents collaborating autonomously to achieve goals.

---

## MCP

Model Context Protocol

A standardized protocol enabling AI models to securely interact with external tools and data sources.

---

## Responsible AI

Developing AI systems that are fair, transparent, safe, secure, and accountable.
